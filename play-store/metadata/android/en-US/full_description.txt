Music Player is a practice tool for musicians, right in your pocket. Slow down the hard parts, change the key to fit your voice, reduce the vocals to sing along, and repeat a section until it is smooth. Everything in one app, with no account and no ads.

WHO IT IS FOR
Guitarists, pianists, bassists, drummers, singers, music teachers, choirs, worship teams, and anyone who wants to practice along with a song.

PRACTICE TOOLS
• Tempo from 0.25x to 2x without changing pitch. Slow down a fast solo, then speed it up little by little.
• Key (transpose) up or down by up to 12 semitones without changing speed.
• Minus One: reduces sound in the center of the mix (usually the vocals). Three modes: Basic, Keep bass, and Vocal focus, with adjustable strength.
• A-B loop: mark a start and an end and repeat it.
• 5-band equalizer.
• Metronome with a count-in before the song starts.
• Per-song presets: the last tempo and key are remembered.

YOUR MUSIC
• In-app library: add MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, or FLAC files.
• Music on phone: browse the music already on your phone by folder, including the SD card.
• Queue, shuffle, repeat, and auto next.
• The last song and position are remembered when you reopen the app.
• Playback keeps going with the screen off, with controls in the notification and on the lock screen.

SEARCH LEGAL MUSIC
Find and download music licensed under Creative Commons or in the public domain from the Internet Archive, Openverse, and Wikimedia Commons. The creator and license are saved with the song.

PRIVACY
• No account, no tracking, no ads.
• Your music and settings stay on your phone. We run no server that receives data from the app.
• The internet is used only when you use Search.
• Minimal permissions: music and audio to read songs on your phone, and notifications for playback controls.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Minus One is a simple reduction, not AI vocal separation. Results vary by recording, and mono recordings become silent.
• The license shown in search results is the uploader's claim. Check the source page if in doubt.
• Open source (GPL-3.0).
